All-terrane dog

The main mean of transportation in our 2015 mapping field word was all-terrane vehicle. A huge noisy machine that by its outlook is more suitable for military could cross the tundra and was much cheaper compared to helicopter.

Bear on Shiveluch

We were climbing the western remnant of the old Shiveluch edifice when a pair of brown bears threw themselves towards us. It was scary! Though, turned out that the bears – a mother and a quite grown up cub – were even more scared. By accident, we pushed them towards the cliff, an they just did not know where to go! On this photo you can see the heels of a cub – he/she was running for their life!

Tolbachik lava cave

One of the multiple lava caves formed during 2012–2013 Tolbachik fissure eruption. Durng the eruption, these caves were the chanels through which lave flowed. Where the roof of the channels collapsed, skylights formed. The entrance into this cave was one of these skylights. In 2018, when the photo was taken, the temperature in some caves still exceeded 80ºC (though in this one it was ≈30ºC – still much warmer than +5ºC outside).
