CV & Publications

Anton Kutyrev, PhD

Lecturer in Exploration Geology, Cardiff University Kazakhstan, Astana, Kazakhstan.

Research focus: Petrology and mineralogy of mafic-ultramafic and island-arc systems; melt-rock and fluid-rock interaction; origin of chromitites; platinum-group element mineralogy from hand-sample to nanoscale, including alloys and PGE oxides; PGE and PGM cycling and mobility in melts and fluids in ultramafic rocks and layered intrusions; oxygen isotopes as tracers of crustal recycling and early Earth surface environments; critical minerals in the context of the energy transition.

Education

  • 2020

    PhD in geology and mineral sciences. Awarded in 2020 after the 2019 defence through the Dissertation Council at the V.S. Sobolev Institute of Geology and Mineralogy, SB RAS. Research was conducted at the Institute of Volcanology and Seismology, RAS, Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky.

  • 2017

    Specialist degree with distinction in applied geology, Saint Petersburg Mining University, Russia. Five-year full-time programme, broadly equivalent to a Master of Science.

Academic appointments

  • Current

    Lecturer in Exploration Geology, Cardiff University Kazakhstan, Astana, Kazakhstan.

    Research and teaching in economic and exploration geology, including layered intrusions, chromitites, platinum-group elements, critical minerals, and isotope geochemistry.

    Current duties combine teaching, programme leadership, curriculum development, and research in economic and exploration geology. I lead the Exploration Geology programme on the Kazakhstan side and coordinate its local delivery. I am also organising the programme's first fieldwork activities and developing a new field course from scratch, including its academic structure, field-based learning, and teaching materials.

    I teach World of Dynamic Environments (EA3504), a Year 1 module that uses geological and environmental case studies to introduce students to dynamic Earth systems. This year, I will also teach Earth Materials and fieldwork.

  • 2025 to 2026

    Postdoctoral Research Fellow, School of Geosciences, University of the Witwatersrand, Johannesburg, South Africa.

    Research on critical minerals in layered mafic-ultramafic intrusions and Bushveld chromitites within the DSTI CIMERA industry–public consortium, with a focus on Ni-Cu-PGE systems, chromitites, and the petrogenesis of the Critical Zone.

  • 2023 to 2025

    Research Associate, School of Earth and Environmental Sciences, Cardiff University, Wales, UK.

    Work on critical-mineral and PGE geochemistry in layered intrusions and related volcanic rocks as part of the Horizon Europe SEMACRET consortium on energy-transition mineral systems, in an interdisciplinary team spanning geology, geophysics, and social sciences.

  • 2022 to 2023

    Fulbright Visiting Scholar, School of Earth Sciences, University of Oregon, USA.

    Research on Early Archean climate using triple oxygen and hydrogen isotopes in 3.8 Ga metalavas from the Saglek-Hebron terrane, including development of isotope fractionation models.

  • 2020 to 2022

    Research Associate, Institute of Volcanology and Seismology, Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky, Russia.

    Research on PGE mineralization and hydrothermal processes in subduction-related magmatic systems in Kamchatka.

  • 2017 to 2020

    PhD candidate, Institute of Volcanology and Seismology, Petropavlovsk-Kamchatsky, Russia.

    Doctoral work on the geology and platinum mineralization of concentrically zoned dunite-clinopyroxenite-gabbro complexes of the Tamanvayam and Epilchik groups.

  • 2012 to 2017

    Engineer, 0.5 FTE, Karpinsky Russian Geological Institute, Saint Petersburg, Russia.

    Work on regional geology and petrology, including petrography and geochemical data for igneous and metamorphic rocks. Work on rock collections from Academician F.N. Chernyshev Central Geological Research Museum, and preparation of a field rock identification guide.

Analytical and modelling skills

  • Hands-on: SEM-EDS, Raman spectroscopy, field portable XRF, clean-lab chemistry and mass spectrometry for Rb-Sr, Sm-Nd, and triple-oxygen isotopes using a laser fluorination line, and LA-ICP-MS.

  • Data reduction and interpretation: EBSD, X-ray diffraction, synchrotron micro-XRF/XAS, FIB-TEM, EPMA, and atom-probe tomography.

  • Modelling and data processing: Python and spreadsheet-based workflows, MELTS- and MAGEMin-based thermodynamic modelling, Monte Carlo modelling of triple-oxygen isotope exchange, and mass-balance and mixing models.
