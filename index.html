Anton Kutyrev

Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of the Witwatersrand

I work on layered intrusions, chromitites, platinum-group elements, and isotope geochemistry. My research moves between field geology, petrology, geochemistry, microanalysis, and modelling. Current projects involve Bushveld Complex, inclusions in Cr-spinel, Ordovician layered intrusions of Wales, behavior of platinum-group elements during differentiation of basaltic magmas, and nanometer-scale ore mineralogy.

I have worked across Kamchatka, Urals, South Africa, Wales, USA, and Tasmania. Right now, I am based in the beautiful country of South Africa.
