Meet people

Conferences

A conference is the fastest way to meet the people you might later work for. Students get reduced fees, and most meetings have travel support (see Funding).

MDSG

Mineral Deposits Studies Group. A small UK winter meeting on ore deposits. About half of the talks are by students, and there are student prizes.

MDSG meetings

SGA Biennial Meeting

The main European meeting on mineral deposits, held every two years with field trips and short courses. Next: Perth, 2027.

SGA meetings

SEG Conference

The Society of Economic Geologists conference. Ore deposits and exploration, with a strong industry presence.

SEG events

EGU General Assembly

Vienna, every spring. Huge and covers all of Earth science. Good for a first poster.

EGU General Assembly

Goldschmidt

The main geochemistry conference. Worth it if your project involves geochemistry or isotopes.

Goldschmidt grants

Money

Funding

Small grants for research costs and travel. Most of them want a short proposal and a letter from a supervisor, so ask early.

SEG

Research grants for students in economic geology, plus fellowships for first-year graduate students.

SEG student funding

SGA Mobility Grant

Pays for travel and analyses at a lab run by an SGA member.

SGA Mobility Grant

GSA research grants

For Master's and PhD research in any area of geology.

GSA grants

EGU travel support

Roland Schlich travel support: free registration and help with travel to the EGU General Assembly. You must be the first author and presenter.

EGU funding guide

Goldschmidt grants

Grants for students to attend Goldschmidt, mainly for students from lower- and middle-income countries.
