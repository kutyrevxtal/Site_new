23AK127

Chromitite stringers at the footwall of the Upper Chromitite, Akanvaara layered intrusion, Lapland, Finland. Thin section is oriented, and the left side corresponds to higher stratigraphy. The section comprises Cr-spinel (opaque), elongated clinopyroxene crystals almost entirely converted into actinolite (colourless to green), and biotite (brown). Biotite appears exactly at the same level as the largest Cr-spinel segregation that forms the matrix of chromitite and the overlying silicate rock. This feature is pretty unique for Akanvaara and nearby coeval Koitelainen intrusions. Large thin section, ≈10×2.2 cm.
